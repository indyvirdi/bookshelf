The core idea fits on a sticky note: make good habits obvious and easy, make bad ones invisible and awkward. What makes the book useful is how practical it gets about the environment around a habit rather than willpower.

It repeats itself in the second half, but the chapters on habit stacking and identity are worth coming back to.
